Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Planning a Kashmir trip raises real questions. Here are honest answers.

Ask Us on WhatsApp
What is the best time to visit Kashmir? +
April to June is peak season — meadows are green, Gulmarg has remnant snow, and weather is ideal for families. July to September brings the famous Kashmir summer. October is shoulder season with stunning autumn colours. December to February is for snow lovers — Gulmarg transforms into a ski destination, though some roads may close.
Is Kashmir safe to visit in 2026? +
Kashmir has seen significant improvement in ground conditions. Tourist footfall is at record highs. As a Srinagar-based operator, we monitor conditions daily. If a route is not safe on a given day, we change it — we advise travellers the same way we would advise our own families.
How do I book a Kashmir tour from my city? +
WhatsApp us at +91-91495 62552 with your travel dates, number of people, and what kind of trip you have in mind. We build a draft itinerary within 24 hours — no commitment required. We have packages from Kolkata, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, and other cities.
What does a Kashmir tour package typically include? +
Airport pickup and drop, accommodation (hotel or houseboat), daily breakfast and dinner, all sightseeing by private cab, an English-speaking local guide, Shikara ride on Dal Lake, and 24/7 on-ground support. Adventure activities and air travel are added separately.
Do you customise packages for specific budgets? +
Yes. Whether you are planning a budget trip at ₹12,000 per person or a luxury houseboat experience at ₹40,000+, we work with your number. Share your budget and we build the best possible itinerary within it — no pressure to upgrade.
Do you offer Leh Ladakh + Kashmir combined tours? +
Yes — one of our most popular itineraries. A Leh Ladakh + Kashmir combined tour typically runs 8–10 days, covering Pangong Lake, Nubra Valley, Khardung La, and then transitioning to the Kashmir valley for Gulmarg, Dal Lake, and Pahalgam.
